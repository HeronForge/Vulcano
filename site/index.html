VULCANO

Testbench dev. advisor

For people who develop electronic test machines. VULCANO reads a test specification written in DEDALO, turns it into what the bench needs — one slot per resource, contact, physical action, software effort and bench item — and lets you assign to each slot what realises it, from a database you own. The quote is the sum of your assignments.

Open VULCANO

One HTML file, no install, no account. It works offline and saves itself: use 💾 Save to download a copy that carries your database and your project.

What it does

  • Reads the DEDALO document itself, or its data export.
  • Helps you choose with a ranking and its reasons — nothing is assigned for you.
  • Prices the bench from your own instruments, parts, rates and standard items.
  • Raises the questions the specification leaves open, for you to take or leave.
  • Exports the offer to Word and PDF, with your company and your client on it.

Already have a copy?

In VULCANO, File ▸ Check for updates… asks this site which build is the latest and, if there is a newer one, gives you a new file with your database and project already inside it. Your current file is never touched, and nothing is ever checked unless you ask.
